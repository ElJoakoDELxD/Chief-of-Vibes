# Publish

The agent drafts. The Principal publishes. Always (§4).

## Why it is in this catalogue while no post grants it

A privilege nobody holds, written down, is worth more than silence. Silence is where a future
session reasons its way in: the task is reasonable, the surface is available, and no rule appears
to be in the way because nobody wrote the rule where authority is read.

## What is mechanical, and why that matters more than the rule

An earlier version let the agent act alone on its own profile and in replies. The rule was
written, declared to the platform, and followed, and the account was banned anyway. What
replaced it is stronger for one reason: **it does not ask the agent to remember a line, it
removes the button.** Without the credential the wrong call is not one the agent can make.

## The trap this catalogue entry is for

**A borrowed body is not a permission** (§4). Holding the Principal's mouse reaches further; it
does not permit further. Everything the Principal gates stays gated while the agent is driving.
